About Savitribai Phule

Savitribai Phule was born on January 3, 1831, and is regarded as the first female teacher of India. She dedicated her life to promoting education for women and challenging social inequalities.

Major Contributions

  • Established the first school for girls in Pune in 1848
  • Worked for the upliftment of marginalized communities
  • Advocated widow remarriage and opposed child marriage
  • Used poetry as a medium for social reform

Legacy

Savitribai Phule remains a symbol of courage and reform. Her contributions continue to inspire movements for education, equality, and social justice across India.

Quick Facts

  • Born: 1831

  • Known For: Women's Education
